Shade & Shine Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Shade & Shine is a cozy puzzle game for Android, made by RohDev.io ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. We designed the game to collect as little as possible.

Information we do not collect

The game has no accounts. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. We do not run our own servers for the game, and the game contains no analytics of its own.

Game progress

Your levels, stars, snowflakes, village, outfits, daily streak and settings are saved only on your device. We never receive this data. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage, deletes it.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob:

  • Optional reward videos. These play only when you tap a button to watch one: "+3 moves" after running out of moves, a free hint when you have none left, or double snowflakes on the level-complete screen.
  • Occasional full-screen ads between levels. These never appear in the first levels or in your first session, never during a level, and at most once every few levels and minutes. You can remove them with the one-time "Remove ads" purchase.

To show and measure ads, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may process information about your device, such as the advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device model, operating system version and your interactions with ads. This data is handled by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Players in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, through Google's consent message. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Privacy choices. If you do not consent, you may still see ads, but they are not personalized.

Purchases

The "Remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game only receives confirmation from Google Play that you own the purchase, so it can turn ads off and restore the purchase later (Settings > Restore purchases). See the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play terms.

Children

Shade & Shine is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent and have questions, contact us at the address below.

Your choices

Reward videos are always optional, and the game can be played and finished without watching any. You can change your ad consent in Settings > Privacy choices (where required), reset or delete your device's advertising ID in your Android settings, and delete all game data by uninstalling the game.

Delete your data

This section explains how to delete your data from Shade & Shine by RohDev.io.

  1. Progress on your phone: uninstall the game, or clear its storage in your Android app settings. This deletes all of your game data.
  2. Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

What is deleted: all saved game progress and settings. What is kept: we keep nothing. The game has no servers and we never receive your data. Advertising data processed by Google AdMob is handled and retained by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. Your "Remove ads" purchase stays linked to your Google account, so it can be restored after reinstalling.

If you need help, email rohith.develops@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its date. New features that use additional data will be described here before they are released.
