OrgMate Privacy Policy

OrgMate for Salesforce: All-in-One Toolkit (Chrome extension) · Developer: RohDev.io · Last updated: November 24, 2026

OrgMate is a browser extension that helps Salesforce admins and developers work with their own Salesforce orgs. This policy explains what data the extension handles and how. In short: OrgMate has no servers. It does not collect, store, sell or share your data with the developer or any third party.

Data OrgMate handles

  • Salesforce session. To call the Salesforce APIs on your behalf, OrgMate reads the Salesforce session cookie (sid) that your browser already holds for the org you are logged in to. It is used only inside your browser, only to send requests directly to that same Salesforce org, and is never stored by OrgMate or sent anywhere else.
  • Salesforce data. Records, metadata, code, users, logs and limits you view or change through OrgMate travel only between your browser and your Salesforce org. OrgMate does not copy them to any other place.
  • Current page address. OrgMate reads the address of the Salesforce page you are on to show context, for example the record you are viewing. It is not stored or sent anywhere.

Data stored on your device

OrgMate saves the following in your browser's local extension storage so the extension remembers your preferences. It stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall OrgMate:

  • Settings: theme, accent colour, sidebar order, launcher position and panel width, safety confirmations.
  • Per-org display settings you choose: colour, label, banner and favicon options.
  • Query history, saved queries, Apex script history and recently used items in Find and Import.

You can clear history at any time in OrgMate Settings, or remove everything by uninstalling the extension.

What OrgMate does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third-party service.
  • No data is sold, rented or transferred, and none is used for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose, or to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
  • No remote code: all of OrgMate's code ships inside the extension package.

Permissions

  • cookies: read the Salesforce session cookie described above.
  • storage: keep your settings and history on your device.
  • Access to Salesforce domains (for example *.salesforce.com, *.force.com): show the OrgMate sidebar on Salesforce pages and call your org's APIs. OrgMate does not run on other websites.
