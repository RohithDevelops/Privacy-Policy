Privacy Policy

Enhanced Find in Page · Last updated 29 September 2026

Summary

Enhanced Find in Page does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything it does happens inside your browser.

What the extension accesses

To find text, the extension reads the text of the web page you are searching (and, when you use "Search all tabs", the pages open in your other tabs). This text is processed only in your browser, in memory, and is discarded when you close the find bar or the page. It is never sent to the developer or to any third party.

What the extension stores

Using Chrome's built-in extension storage, the extension saves:

  • your settings (for example theme, find-bar position and default search options), synced through your own Chrome profile if Chrome Sync is enabled;
  • your recent search terms (up to 30) and last-used search options, stored locally on your device.

Removing the extension deletes all of this data. Settings → Reset to defaults restores the default settings.

What the extension does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or remote logging.
  • No network requests to the developer or third parties.
  • No remote code: all code ships inside the extension package.

Permissions

  • storage: save settings and search history as described above.
  • scripting: add the find bar to tabs that were already open when the extension was installed, and run "Search all tabs".
  • Access to all sites: needed so the find bar and the Ctrl/Cmd+F shortcut work on any page you choose to search.
