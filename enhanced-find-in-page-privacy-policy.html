Privacy Policy: Enhanced Find in Page

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Enhanced Find in Page does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no analytics, no tracking, no remote code and no servers.

What the extension accesses and why

  • Page content. To find and highlight text, the extension reads the text of the page you are searching, including text shown in form fields (typed text, placeholders, buttons and dropdowns; never password fields). This happens only inside your browser. Page content is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Other open tabs. Only when you use "Search all tabs", the extension reads the text of your open tabs to count matches and show snippets. The results stay in your browser.
  • Stored on your device:
    • Your settings, including the light/dark theme you pick in the find bar. These are kept in the browser's extension sync storage, so if you've turned on browser sync, your browser may sync them between your own devices.
    • Your recent searches and your last search (used to fill in the find bar in other tabs), only if history is on. These stay on this device only, and are never saved in private/incognito windows. Turning history off removes the saved last search.
    • The find bar's position.
    • The current search for an open tab, so the bar can reopen after a reload. This is kept in session storage and cleared when the tab or browser closes.

You can clear your search history at any time on the Settings page, and removing the extension deletes all of its data.

Permissions

  • Read and change data on all websites (<all_urls>): needed to replace Ctrl/Cmd+F and to search and highlight text on any page you visit. Highlights are drawn without changing the page.
  • scripting: used to enable the find bar in tabs that were already open when the extension was installed, and for "Search all tabs".
  • storage: used to save your settings, history and bar position.
