Grab It All Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Grab It All is a casual game for players of all ages, made by RohDev.io ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. We designed the game to collect as little as possible, because children play it too.

Information we do not collect

The game has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. We do not run our own servers for the game.

Game progress

Your progress, coins, outfits and settings are saved only on your device. We cannot see this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game can show an optional video ad when you choose "Watch & Continue" after losing all your hearts. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. Because the game is played by children, every ad request is marked as child-directed and as coming from a user under the age of consent, and only ads rated suitable for general audiences (rated G) are requested. This means the ads are not personalized and are not based on your interests or behavior.

To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process limited device information, such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version and basic interactions with the ad. This data is handled by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Players in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland may be shown Google's consent message where required by law.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game follows Google Play's Families Policy, and its ads are limited as described above. Parents can contact us at the address below with any questions.

Your choices

Watching ads is always optional. You can reset or delete your device's advertising ID in your Android settings. You can delete all game data by uninstalling the game.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its date. New features that use additional data, such as an optional Google sign-in for cloud saves, will be described here before they are released.
